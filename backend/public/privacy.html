Privacy Policy

Omega Portfolio Tracker · Last updated October 20, 2026

In short: Omega has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your transactions stay on your iPhone unless you turn on the optional cloud backup.

Who we are

Omega Portfolio Tracker ("Omega") is published by 92labs, Montréal, Québec, Canada. Contact: 92labs320@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The transactions you enter (asset, type, quantity, price, fee, date, platform and notes) are stored in a local database on your iPhone. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or any account credentials. Omega never connects to your brokerage or exchange accounts and never holds or moves assets.

Optional cloud backup

If you turn on Cloud backup in Settings, a copy of your transactions is sent over HTTPS to our server (hosted by Vercel) and stored in a PostgreSQL database operated by Neon. The backup is linked only to a random key generated on your iPhone and kept in the iOS Keychain; our server stores a one-way hash of that key, not the key itself, and no personal identifier. You can turn the backup off at any time and delete it permanently with Settings → Delete cloud backup. Deleting the app does not delete the backup automatically, so delete it in the app first if you want it removed.

Market data requests

  • Crypto prices are requested by your device directly from CoinGecko. CoinGecko receives your IP address and the coins requested, as with any website visit (CoinGecko privacy policy).
  • Stock prices are requested through our server, which forwards only the ticker symbols to Finnhub. Finnhub does not receive your IP address.

Server logs

Like any web service, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (IP address, time, request path) for security and debugging. We do not use them to identify or profile you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.

Retention and your rights

Local data stays until you erase it (Settings → Erase data on this iPhone) or delete the app. Cloud backup data stays until you delete it in the app, or until you ask us to delete it by email. Because the backup is anonymous, we can only locate it from your device, so the in-app deletion is the most reliable method. You can export your transactions as CSV at any time.

Children

Omega is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.

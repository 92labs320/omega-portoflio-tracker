Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Omega works without sign-up. Your data is stored on your iPhone.

How do I back up or move my data?
Turn on Settings → Cloud backup. You can also export a CSV file from Settings at any time.

How is profit and loss calculated?
Omega uses the average-cost method. Fees are added to the cost of buys and deducted from sale proceeds. Unrealized P&L uses the latest available price.

Why is a price missing?
Prices come from CoinGecko (crypto) and Finnhub (stocks). If a provider is unreachable or does not list an asset, Omega shows "no price" instead of an estimate.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete cloud backup removes the server copy; Settings → Erase data on this iPhone removes local data.
